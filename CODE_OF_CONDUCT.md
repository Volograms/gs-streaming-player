# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone. Be respectful,
assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and make space for different
levels of experience and accessibility needs.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, doxxing, or sustained disruption. Maintainers may edit or remove content and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the security/contact channel listed by the
repository owner. Reports will be handled as confidentially and promptly as practical.
